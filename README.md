# Idea

Ok you, yep you are Weak (not harassment!), and you want to be better. So what you want to do to be better? You want to do your best to be better. You want climb to the PEAK.

## Mechanics 

Genre is platformer. And this is a pure platformer without climbing on walls, whatever. But what you can do is upgrade. When you reach next checkpoint you're growing up. 

## Difficulty

I don't really know how hard it is. But for me, it's at least tricky. My last run was about 8 minutes, but before i did 3 minutes.

## Goal

Your goal is to deliver order. From Tyler to... Tyler (i didn't had enough time 😭)

## Control

It's very simple, just like A, D (or arrows) to run, Space to jump and E to interact (also you can get hint to interact if your mouse enter Tyler)
